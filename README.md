## Part 1: CMS PBJ Data Exercise

### Business Context
Clipboard Health is a nationwide staffing platform providing temporary healthcare workers to long-term care facilities.

The **Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)** publishes the **Payroll Based Journal (PBJ) Daily Nurse Staffing** dataset on a quarterly basis. This dataset reports daily staffing hours for all registered nursing homes in the United States.

**Key notes:**
- Staffing includes both **employees** (full-time) and **contractors** (temporary)
- All Clipboard Health workers are classified as **contractors**
- Analysis focuses on **2024 Q1**, the most recent available quarter

---

### Objectives
- Identify facilities and regions with high reliance on contractor staffing
- Surface patterns that indicate staffing shortages or sales opportunities
- Provide **data-backed recommendations** to Clipboard Health sales leadership
